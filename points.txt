-> Production & Inventory (for b2b manufacturing / trading garments company)
- Can you tell your total production quantity for today in under 5 seconds?
- Which design sold the most units last month?
- Which design is currently occupying the most inventory?
- Which SKU hasn't moved in the last 60 days?
- How many pieces are currently stuck in production?
- Which order is running behind schedule right now?
- How many pieces were rejected during quality checks this week?
- Which product gives the highest profit per piece?


-> Sales & Orders (for actual brands / shop)
- Which customer generated the most revenue this month?
- Which customer has the highest outstanding payment?
- How many orders are delayed today?
- Which sales channel is performing best right now?
- Can you see pending orders without calling anyone?
- How many repeat customers purchased this month?


-> Team & Operations - overall
- If your production manager takes leave tomorrow, can someone else understand everything in 10 minutes?
- How many times is the same order data entered by different people?
- How many WhatsApp messages are exchanged before an order gets dispatched?
- How many phone calls does it take to know the status of a customer order?
- How many Excel files are being maintained for the same information?

Case Study Angles

-> Case Study 1
A garment manufacturer was tracking production across WhatsApp, Excel and phone calls.
Order status took 20-30 minutes to verify.
We built a centralized system where production, inventory and dispatch status were available instantly.


-> Case Study 2
A ladies garment wholesaler had no visibility into which designs were actually making money.
Best-selling products and dead stock were identified manually.
We built a dashboard that highlighted fast-moving and slow-moving inventory automatically.


-> Case Study 3
A garment business depended heavily on one operations manager.
When he wasn't available, nobody knew the exact status of production and dispatch.
We digitized the workflow so information lived in the system, not in one person's head.
