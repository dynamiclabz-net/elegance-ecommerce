About this piece

The Ivory Embroidered Salwar Set is a testament to the timeless artistry of Indian textile craftsmanship. This ethereal three-piece ensemble — comprising an intricately embroidered kurta, matching palazzo, and sheer dupatta — is designed for the woman who moves through the world with quiet elegance.

Crafted from premium georgette with a delicate hand-embroidered bodice, the kurta features subtle threadwork in ivory and champagne tones that catch the light beautifully. The wide-leg palazzo in soft cotton ensures all-day comfort without compromising on style.

Whether you are attending a family celebration, a sophisticated evening gathering, or a festive occasion, this set effortlessly bridges the traditional and the contemporary.

Hand Wash Only

Gently hand wash in cold water with mild detergent. Do not machine wash.

Do Not Bleach

Avoid chlorine bleach or harsh chemicals that may damage embroidery threads.

Dry in Shade

Lay flat to dry in a shaded area. Avoid direct sunlight to preserve colour.

Low Iron

Iron on reverse at low heat. Use a pressing cloth over embroidered sections.

Store Carefully

Store folded in a cotton bag. Avoid plastic packaging that traps moisture.

No Dry Clean

We recommend against dry cleaning as solvents may affect the delicate finish.
